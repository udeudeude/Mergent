NOTES ON AN IMPOSSIBLE OBJECT

The real cube is the controller.

This app is only for the physical MERGE Cube. Point the iPhone's rear camera at one of its six marked faces. When a face is recognized, Mergent overlays a CGI cube whose visible face appears hollow, containing one of six procedural specimens.

Turn the real cube to change chambers. Disturb agitates the current specimen. Breach introduces a visitor from a neighboring chamber. Adjust Improbability to change their behavior, or make a Postcard of the camera scene.

Mergent automatically prepares its six face patterns and combines measurements from adjacent visible faces into one smoothed cube pose. This approximates the multi-face geometry used by native apps, but the Safari tracking implementation remains experimental.
