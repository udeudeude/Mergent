NOTES ON A DISOBEDIENT GEOMETRY

Only the face you see can be empty.

Scan MERGE Cube opens your rear camera and recognizes the markings on a physical MERGE Cube. The cube is visually replaced by CGI: only the face toward you looks hollow, revealing an impossible room. Turn the real cube to change what appears inside.

Virtual Preview works without the physical cube. Drag the CGI cube with a finger or mouse; the outward-facing side becomes the open room.

Tap a room symbol to rotate directly to it. Disturb agitates the inhabitant. Breach pulls a neighboring chamber's inhabitant into the opening, making a temporary hybrid. The Improbability slider changes how the exhibits behave.

Collect Postcard captures the current encounter as a PNG. Sounds are synthesized locally. No account or upload is needed. For camera tracking, the six face markers are compiled once from MERGE’s official downloadable PDF and saved on this device.

ONE-TIME / PHYSICAL CUBE TRACKING

Give the camera six eyes.

The CGI cube needs to recognize the real cube’s six patterns. Instead of photographing them, Mergent can read MERGE's official printable PDF and learn all six directly on your iPhone. Your PDF is not uploaded.

First-time setup may require some processing. Leave this panel open while the six faces are learned.

If the PDF cannot be fetched directly, save the PDF using the link above and choose Import PDF from Files. You only need to do this once per browser. This is experimental face-by-face image tracking, not MERGE's proprietary full-cube tracker.
